CS101: Introduction to Computer Science

Credits: 3

Instructor: Dr. Nguyen Van A (nguyenvana@hcmiu.edu)

Schedule: Mondays & Wednesdays, 09:00 AM - 10:30 AM

Location: A2, Room 101

Course Description

An entry-level introduction to core computer science principles. Topics include basic problem-solving, algorithmic thinking, data representation, pseudocode design, and an overview of computing history.

Prerequisites

None. Open to all majors.
