Welcome to the Computer Science Department

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science. We are dedicated to delivering world-class education and fostering cutting-edge research in software engineering, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and systems infrastructure. Our curriculum is designed to empower students with both theoretical foundations and practical skills.

Our department offers undergraduate and graduate programs tailored to modern industry demands. Through hands-on laboratory work, collaborative projects, and industry partnerships, our students build strong problem-solving skill sets that prepare them for top tech careers and academia.

Explore our site to meet our distinguished faculty members or contact us directly to learn more about our academic offerings and upcoming admissions cycles.
